# Morocco Portfolio Market Research

Research date: 5 September 2026

## Recommendation

Position the portfolio as an evidence-led **SEO + Maps + AI Search Visibility** practice for independent Moroccan hospitality and experience businesses. Start with riads, boutique hotels, tours, surf camps, restaurants, and other operators whose revenue depends on being discovered and trusted online.

The recommended promise is:

> I help independent Moroccan hospitality brands earn more direct inquiries and bookings through Google, Maps, and AI-assisted search.

This positioning is an inference from the evidence below. No reliable public dataset establishes Moroccan SEO-service spending or willingness to pay, so the offer must be validated through interviews and paid diagnostics.

## Why this market

- Morocco's Ministry of Tourism reports 19.8 million tourist arrivals in 2025, up 14% from 2024, alongside 43.4 million nights in classified accommodation and MAD 138 billion in tourism receipts. This indicates a large and growing commercial market with international demand. [Ministry of Tourism: key figures](https://mtaess.gov.ma/fr/chiffres-cles/)
- OMPIC reports 56,572 business creations in the first half of 2025, up 17.7%. Commerce, property/construction, services, and hotels/restaurants were strongly represented. [OMPIC business-creation figures](https://www.ompic.ma/fr/actualites/office-marocain-de-la-propriete-industrielle-et-commerciale-les-creations-dentreprises)
- ANRT reported 39.9 million internet subscriptions at the end of March 2025. Its household research also supports a strongly mobile-oriented market. [ANRT Q1 2025 memo](https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2025-06/Memo%20T1-2025.pdf)
- Tourism discovery is naturally multilingual, local, review-sensitive, and conversion-oriented. Businesses must compete in ordinary search, Maps, travel platforms, and increasingly AI-assisted research. This makes the sector unusually suitable for visible case studies and measurable commercial outcomes.

## Audience and language decision

Use French as the commercial and editorial base for the first six months. Publish English versions of the homepage, core services, flagship studies, and tourism-facing work. Add Arabic selectively after query and customer evidence shows which pages deserve it.

Do not automatically translate every article. Each maintained language version should have its own URL and reciprocal `hreflang` annotations. Google recommends explicit localized URLs and advises against relying on IP-based language adaptation. [Google multilingual-site guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions)

Darija can be effective in social content, interviews, examples, and conversion tests, but a large romanized-Darija search section should wait for Search Console or keyword evidence.

## Competitive implication

Moroccan consultants and agencies already advertise SEO, AEO, and GEO. The acronyms are not a defensible differentiator. The open position is a transparent Morocco search lab built from:

1. Original local datasets and field studies
2. Reproducible experiments
3. Public teardowns and implementation details
4. Case studies tied to inquiries, bookings, and revenue
5. Honest reporting of failures, limitations, and uncertainty

Market the outcome as **search visibility and direct demand**. Explain AEO/GEO/LLMO inside educational pages rather than making the homepage a list of unfamiliar abbreviations.

## AI-search evidence and limits

Google states that the same foundational SEO requirements apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode and that there are no extra technical requirements for inclusion. Pages must be indexable, snippet-eligible, useful, reliable, and accessible. [Google: AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

Structured data can help search engines understand content and enable eligible rich results, but it does not guarantee display or ranking. [Google structured-data introduction](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/intro-structured-data)

Therefore:

- Do not promise to “rank in ChatGPT.”
- Do not sell special AI schema as a shortcut.
- Improve crawlability, source quality, entity clarity, factual consistency, original evidence, and third-party authority.
- Measure a controlled set of prompts over time, but label the results observational because answers vary by date, model, location, and account state.
- Pair AI-citation monitoring with Search Console, analytics, calls, forms, WhatsApp conversations, bookings, and revenue.

## Service opportunity

The strongest service ladder is:

1. A paid discovery visibility audit covering the site, Business Profile, competitors, multilingual content, conversion path, and selected AI-search prompts.
2. A fixed-scope local discovery sprint implementing the highest-value fixes.
3. An ongoing direct-demand retainer focused on content, local authority, technical upkeep, measurement, and conversion.
4. A premium multilingual market-entry engagement for tourism businesses targeting France, Spain, the UK, or other source markets.

Suggested pricing in the execution plan is a hypothesis, not a market fact. It should be tested with five paid diagnostics before being treated as validated.

## Content opportunity

The first major asset should be a 100-property study of Marrakech hotel and riad search visibility. It can produce multiple differentiated outputs:

- Google Maps and website-quality benchmarks
- Direct-booking and OTA-dependence observations
- French/English localization findings
- Technical SEO patterns
- AI-assistant citation tests
- Individual outreach to every included property
- A reusable scorecard that becomes the paid audit method

Supporting content should focus on sector playbooks, public experiments, buyer decision guides, multilingual SEO, and strong opinions grounded in Moroccan examples. Generic definitions and mass-produced articles will not create an advantage.

Google's people-first guidance emphasizes original information, research, analysis, firsthand expertise, and clear sourcing. Its spam policies warn against scaled low-value content created primarily to manipulate rankings. [Google helpful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) and [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)

## Technical direction

Astro is an appropriate fit for a content-heavy static-first site because it minimizes client-side JavaScript and supports built-in internationalized routing. [Astro internationalization documentation](https://docs.astro.build/en/recipes/i18n/)

Cloudflare officially supports Astro deployments on Workers. [Cloudflare Astro guide](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/framework-guides/web-apps/astro/)

Use Markdown/MDX content collections, Git-based publishing, responsive images, accessible semantic HTML, an XML sitemap, RSS, canonical URLs, locale annotations, and JSON-LD that accurately reflects visible content. Add a CMS only when a nontechnical publishing workflow genuinely requires it.

## Key uncertainties to validate

- Will tourism operators pay the proposed audit and sprint prices?
- Is French the highest-converting commercial language for the chosen cities and operator types?
- Which segment has the shortest path from visibility problem to measurable revenue?
- Does AI-search language improve sales conversations, or does it distract from direct booking outcomes?
- Can the first research study earn links, replies, and qualified calls?

The first 90 days should answer these questions with five operator interviews, five paid diagnostics, tracked conversions, and one original market study. Traffic alone is not validation.
