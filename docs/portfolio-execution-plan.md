# Morocco Portfolio and Organic Growth Plan

## Executive decision

Build a personal expert brand focused on one commercial promise:

> I help independent Moroccan hospitality and experience businesses earn more direct inquiries and bookings through Google, Google Maps, and AI-assisted search.

The first target market is riads, boutique hotels, tour operators, surf camps, restaurants, and experience providers in Marrakech, Agadir/Taghazout, Essaouira, Tangier, Fez, and Casablanca.

This is a better entry point than a generic “SEO expert in Morocco” position because:

- Morocco recorded 19.8 million tourist arrivals and MAD 138 billion in tourism receipts in 2025, according to the Ministry of Tourism.
- The customer journey is naturally multilingual and heavily dependent on discovery, comparison, maps, reviews, and trusted recommendations.
- Independent operators have a concrete financial reason to win direct demand instead of relying entirely on OTAs and social platforms.
- Results can be demonstrated through rankings, map visibility, direct inquiries, booking-page conversion, and branded-search growth.

The expansion market, after proof exists, is Moroccan local-service SMEs with high-value leads, especially real estate, wellness, professional services, and selected clinics.

## Brand and positioning decisions

### Brand model

Use a personal brand: real name, professional portrait, first-person opinions, transparent methodology, and named case studies. The domain should preferably be `firstname-lastname.ma`. Use a `.com` only if international expansion is an immediate priority or the `.ma` is unavailable.

### Public language

Lead publicly with **SEO + AI Search Visibility**. Use AEO, GEO, and LLMO as supporting vocabulary inside educational content. Do not make “FEO/AIO/AEO/GEO expert” the headline; most buyers do not understand the acronyms, and Google states that its AI search features do not require separate technical optimization beyond sound SEO and people-first content.

### Recommended homepage message

**Headline:** Get discovered before your guests book somewhere else.

**Subheading:** I help independent Moroccan hospitality brands improve their visibility across Google, Maps, and AI search—and turn that visibility into more direct inquiries and bookings.

**Primary CTA:** Request a discovery audit

**Secondary CTA:** See the field studies

### Language strategy

- French is the primary sales and editorial language for the first six months.
- English is used for the homepage, key service pages, major research studies, and content aimed at internationally owned or managed properties.
- Arabic is added after the French/English publishing system is working. Start with high-value evergreen guides, not complete automatic translation.
- Give every localized page its own URL and use reciprocal `hreflang` annotations. Do not auto-redirect visitors by IP.
- Translate only pages that deserve a maintained equivalent. Thin machine-translated duplicates will dilute quality.

## Market need the publication will satisfy

The publication should become the practical source for **how people discover and choose Moroccan places online**. It should publish evidence and opinions that agencies rarely expose publicly:

- Audits of Moroccan search results and Google Maps categories
- Direct-booking and OTA-dependence analyses
- Multilingual search behavior and localization mistakes
- Tests of which Moroccan businesses are cited by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI features
- Reviews of real local websites with specific fixes
- Interviews with operators about where their leads and bookings originate
- Clear opinions about weak digital practices in the Moroccan market

The defensible asset is not “a blog.” It is a growing body of Morocco-specific evidence, examples, and firsthand observations.

## Service ladder

### 1. Discovery Visibility Audit

Fixed-scope entry offer. Review the website, Google Business Profile, local competitors, multilingual pages, technical SEO, booking journey, and a controlled set of AI-search prompts.

- Deliverable: prioritized scorecard, recorded walkthrough, 90-day roadmap
- Suggested starting price: MAD 2,500–4,500
- Purpose: turn readers into paid diagnostic engagements

### 2. Local Discovery Sprint

Four-to-six-week implementation for one location or property.

- Technical fixes and measurement setup
- Google Business Profile improvement
- Core location and service pages
- Review and citation process
- Structured data and internal linking
- Suggested starting price: MAD 8,000–15,000

### 3. Direct Demand Growth Retainer

Ongoing organic growth for businesses that have completed the audit or sprint.

- Search opportunity research
- Two high-quality content assets per month
- On-page and internal-link improvements
- Digital PR/outreach using original data
- Search, Maps, conversion, and AI-citation monitoring
- Suggested starting price: MAD 6,000–12,000 per month, with a three-month minimum

### 4. Multilingual Market Entry Project

Premium project for properties or operators targeting a new source market such as France, Spain, or the UK. Price after discovery; do not publish a low commodity rate.

## SEO and AI-search operating system

### Editorial rules

- Write for one real buyer question per page.
- Put a concise answer or finding near the top, then show evidence and nuance.
- Include firsthand screenshots, observations, interviews, measurements, or experiments in every flagship article.
- Cite primary sources and link to the exact source.
- Show the author, credentials, methodology, publication date, and meaningful update date.
- Label opinions and inferences honestly.
- Use AI for assistance, not mass publication. Every article needs human judgment and Morocco-specific evidence.

### Technical rules

- Unique titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, and social images
- XML sitemap, robots file, RSS feed, and clean semantic HTML
- `hreflang` for maintained translations
- JSON-LD that matches visible content: `Person`, `ProfilePage`, `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, and applicable service/business types
- Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools verification
- Fast responsive images and minimal client-side JavaScript
- Accessible navigation, forms, contrast, headings, and keyboard states
- Core Web Vitals targets: LCP at or below 2.5 seconds, INP at or below 200 ms, and CLS at or below 0.1 at the 75th percentile

### AI-search measurement

Treat AI visibility as an experimental channel, not a guaranteed ranking system.

- Define 30 representative prompts across destination, service, and comparison intent.
- Test monthly in a controlled sheet across major answer engines.
- Record whether the brand is mentioned, cited, accurately described, and linked.
- Track the pages and third-party sources associated with mentions.
- Pair this with ordinary Search Console, analytics, lead, and revenue data.

Do not sell a promise to “rank in ChatGPT.” Sell improved source quality, crawlability, entity clarity, factual consistency, and third-party authority.

## Technology decision

Use:

- Astro with TypeScript for a static-first, content-focused build
- Astro content collections with Markdown/MDX for articles, studies, services, and case studies
- Minimal interactive islands only where necessary
- Cloudflare Workers for hosting and deployments
- A Git-based editorial workflow initially; add a headless CMS only when a nontechnical editor genuinely needs it
- Cloudflare Web Analytics or a privacy-conscious analytics product, plus Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
- A simple serverless contact form protected by Turnstile

This keeps the site fast, cheap, portable, and easy to validate. Astro supports built-in internationalized routing, and Cloudflare officially supports Astro deployments.

## Twelve-week execution plan

### Phase 1 — Position and foundation (week 1)

- Secure the personal `.ma` domain and matching social handles.
- Finalize name, one-sentence promise, biography, portrait, and contact method.
- Interview five hospitality operators to validate language, booking, Maps, and OTA pain points.
- Select 100 businesses for the first visibility study and define a reproducible scoring method.
- Create a one-page brand and editorial guide.

**Exit criterion:** positioning and research methodology are frozen; no design work begins before this.

### Phase 2 — Build the minimum credible site (weeks 2–3)

- Scaffold Astro, localization, content collections, metadata, schema, sitemap, RSS, and analytics.
- Build homepage, about, contact, work index, insights index, and three service pages.
- Create reusable article, study, and case-study templates.
- Add performance, accessibility, and structured-data checks to the build workflow.
- Deploy a preview and test mobile, forms, language switching, canonical URLs, and indexing directives.

**Exit criterion:** the site is technically launchable and every commercial page has a clear CTA.

### Phase 3 — Establish proof (weeks 4–5)

- Publish the 100-property Marrakech study.
- Publish the build-in-public portfolio case study with a true zero baseline.
- Complete two public teardowns with permission or using only public evidence.
- Create the downloadable visibility scorecard and connect it to an email follow-up.
- Publish a transparent “how I measure AI-search visibility” methodology page.

**Exit criterion:** a visitor can see evidence of analytical ability even before client case studies exist.

### Phase 4 — Launch and distribution (weeks 6–8)

- Launch and submit sitemaps to Google and Bing.
- Send the study to the businesses included, tourism associations, local publications, founders, and relevant journalists.
- Turn the study into six LinkedIn posts, two short videos, one carousel, and one email sequence.
- Offer five paid or tightly scoped pilot audits; do not offer unlimited free consulting.
- Publish four supporting articles linked to the study and service pages.

**Exit criterion:** at least 30 relevant people have received personalized outreach and the first discovery calls are booked.

### Phase 5 — Convert evidence into sales (weeks 9–12)

- Deliver pilot audits using a standardized scorecard.
- Convert suitable audits into sprints.
- Publish the first client-approved case study or anonymized outcome report.
- Improve service pages using objections heard on calls.
- Publish two additional high-intent guides and one strong opinion piece.
- Review query, conversion, and prompt-tracking data; choose the next city or vertical based on evidence.

**Exit criterion:** one repeatable acquisition loop exists from study/article → audit → sprint → case study.

## Six-month publishing cadence

- Two substantial articles per month
- One original data study per quarter
- One case study or teardown per month
- One operator interview per month
- Two distribution posts per week derived from existing research
- One content refresh session per month

Quality and distribution take priority over publishing volume.

## Scorecard and targets

### Business metrics

- Qualified inquiries
- Discovery calls booked
- Audit-to-sprint conversion rate
- Proposal value and won revenue in MAD
- Source of every lead

### Search metrics

- Indexed valid pages
- Non-branded impressions and clicks
- Queries in positions 1–10 and 11–20
- Referring domains earned by original research
- Service-page conversion rate
- Google Business Profile actions when client access exists

### Six-month validation targets

These are operating targets, not traffic or ranking guarantees:

- 20–24 genuinely useful indexed pages
- Two original Morocco-specific studies
- Three credible case studies or evidence-based teardowns
- Ten relevant referring domains
- Six to twelve qualified inquiries
- One to three paid clients attributable to the site/content system

## What not to do

- Do not launch as a generic developer/marketer portfolio with twenty unrelated skills.
- Do not target “SEO” nationally with thin listicles from day one.
- Do not publish daily AI-generated articles.
- Do not translate every page automatically.
- Do not claim guaranteed rankings or guaranteed AI citations.
- Do not create fake case studies, testimonials, office locations, or review markup.
- Do not hide prices and scope so completely that every visitor must book a call to understand the offer.
- Do not add a CMS, database, newsletter platform, or complex animation until the core publishing loop works.
