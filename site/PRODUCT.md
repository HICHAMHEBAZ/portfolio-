# Morocco Search Lab

## Product intent

Morocco Search Lab is a personal advisory site and research publication for independent Moroccan hospitality brands. It helps owners of riads, boutique lodges, desert camps, and surf retreats understand how potential guests discover them through Google, Maps, and AI-assisted search.

## Audience

- Moroccan hospitality owners and operators who need more direct demand.
- International operators and investors evaluating Moroccan hospitality visibility.
- Readers seeking evidence-led, bilingual opinions about travel-search behaviour in Morocco.

## Product principles

1. Lead with proof and useful diagnosis, not generic marketing claims.
2. Make Moroccan hospitality specificity visible in every major surface.
3. Put a clear next step beside each piece of expertise.
4. Respect a reader's attention with calm motion, honest caveats, and fast pages.
5. Keep all routes legible and accessible on a 320px screen and with keyboard navigation.

## Personality

Warm, exacting, worldly, and quietly confident. Never a generic agency template, travel brochure, or SaaS dashboard.
