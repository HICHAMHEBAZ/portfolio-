Search does not stop at the first click. Before booking, a future guest compares an address, Maps profile, reviews, photos, destination pages, and sometimes an answer generated by an AI interface.

## The useful question

The goal is not simply to rank first. It is to remove the uncertainty that sends a visitor to an OTA, platform, or competitor.

For an independent riad or hotel, that means clear experience pages, consistent business information, current verifiable proof, and a low-friction inquiry or booking path.

## What this publication will measure

This site will publish Moroccan visibility studies with methodology, limitations, and sources. The aim is to replace generic advice with usable observations.
