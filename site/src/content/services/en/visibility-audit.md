The audit answers one practical question: **what does a future customer find before booking somewhere else?**

It reviews your site, Google Business Profile, local pages, trust signals, content, conversion path, and a small set of AI-assisted search prompts.

## What you receive

- A prioritized diagnostic of the issues constraining discovery and demand.
- A comparison against the most relevant search and Maps competitors.
- A realistic roadmap with clear client-side dependencies.

Search performance and AI citations are never guaranteed. The purpose is to create a useful baseline for the next decision.
