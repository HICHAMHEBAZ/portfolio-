The sprint turns useful recommendations into visible improvements. It prioritizes a small amount of work that can be checked: a page answering a clear intent, a fuller profile, a measurable demand path, or an indexing correction.

## The sprint includes

- Technical fixes that prevent important pages from being understood.
- Improvement of a high-intent service or destination page.
- Consistent essential information across Google and the site.
- A measurement baseline for calls, forms, or WhatsApp inquiries.
