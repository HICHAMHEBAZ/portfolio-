# Atlas Intelligence implementation brief

The Stitch project "Moroccan Hospitality Search Consultancy" is the visual source of truth. Its system is translated into the production Astro site, not copied as a static one-page mockup.

## Visual language

- Warm editorial modernism: matte cream canvas, linen-white modules, midnight ink typography.
- Electric cobalt for primary actions and AI-search signals; imperial wine for substantive editorial and conversion moments; restrained blush for contextual emphasis.
- Rounded pill controls, generous 12-column composition, soft warm ambient depth, and dense but readable research modules.
- Plus Jakarta Sans for display, interface, and body typography.

## Interaction rules

- Motion only reinforces hierarchy: short opacity and transform entrances, link/button lift states, and no looping decorative animation.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing transitions and animations.
- All focus states meet high-visibility keyboard requirements; all touch actions have 44px minimum targets.
